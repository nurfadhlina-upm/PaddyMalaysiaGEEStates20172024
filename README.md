# Paddy Malaysia: AI-Enabled Paddy Intelligence & Digital Twin

**Paddy Malaysia** is an interactive research application for exploring paddy production, yield patterns, environmental conditions, and data-driven intelligence across Malaysia. The platform integrates historical agricultural statistics with geospatial and Earth observation-derived features to support the analysis and visualisation of paddy production at state and regional levels.

The application is developed as part of ongoing research on **AI-enabled precision agriculture, digital twins, climate resilience, and agricultural decision intelligence**.

## 🌾 About the Application

The platform provides an interactive environment for exploring Malaysian paddy data across multiple years and locations. Rather than presenting agricultural statistics as static tables, the application combines agricultural, environmental, spatial, and analytical information within an interactive digital environment.

Depending on the available dataset and application version, the platform supports functions such as:

- visualisation of paddy planted area, production, and yield;
- state-level and temporal comparison of paddy performance;
- interactive Malaysian paddy yield maps;
- integration of satellite/Earth observation and environmental indicators;
- exploration of vegetation indicators such as NDVI;
- analysis of rainfall, temperature, solar radiation, soil, and related biophysical conditions;
- identification of temporal patterns and changes;
- AI/ML-based yield modelling and prediction;
- exploration of resilience and environmental conditions; and
- digital-twin-style representation of the Malaysian paddy production system.

The platform is intended primarily for **research, education, demonstration, and decision-support experimentation**.

## 🧠 Research Direction

This application forms part of a broader research direction towards an **Intelligent Paddy Digital Twin**, where agricultural statistics, Earth observation, environmental data, machine learning, and human knowledge can be integrated to provide continuously evolving agricultural intelligence.

The longer-term research explores capabilities including:

**Observe → Analyse → Predict → Explain → Recommend → Adapt**

This includes research on predictive modelling, climate and environmental resilience, explainable AI, adaptive and transfer learning, and human-in-the-loop agricultural decision support.

## 🛰️ Data

The application integrates multiple types of information, which may include:

- Malaysian paddy statistics;
- planted and harvested area;
- paddy production;
- observed yield;
- satellite-derived vegetation indicators;
- rainfall;
- temperature;
- solar radiation;
- soil characteristics; and
- other geospatial or biophysical indicators.

Earth observation and environmental variables used in the research may be derived using **Google Earth Engine** and relevant satellite or global environmental datasets.

Users should refer to the associated dataset documentation and research publications for the exact data sources, preprocessing procedures, spatial resolution, temporal coverage, and limitations.

## ⚠️ Research Use and Limitations

This application is a research prototype and should not be interpreted as an official agricultural forecasting or operational farm-management system.

Predictions, resilience indicators, AI-generated analyses, and other derived outputs are dependent on the underlying datasets, modelling assumptions, spatial and temporal resolution, and experimental settings.

Users should therefore exercise appropriate domain judgement before using outputs for agricultural, policy, investment, or operational decisions.
